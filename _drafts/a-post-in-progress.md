This is an **example draft**. Drafts in `_drafts/` don't need a date in the filename and aren't published to the site — perfect for works in progress.

Preview drafts locally with:

```bash
bundle exec jekyll serve --drafts
```

When it's ready, move it to `_posts/` with a `YEAR-MONTH-DAY-` prefix on the filename and push. That's the whole workflow.
